It was pouring this morning, so I wasn’t planning to go to the café. But when the rain eased up, I thought, “Well, I might as well go.” Good thing I did! The moment I arrived, I noticed that someone had stolen my plant. 😂

Before the café opened, there was a day when I forgot to bring my bar stool inside. The owner of the shop next door kindly took it into his shop for me. The next day, he told me, “A nice stool like that will definitely get stolen if you leave it outside.”

But who would’ve thought someone would steal a pot of cactus, too? 🌵😂

The owner’s wife said, “No one would want a cactus. Maybe they just liked the pot.”

I said, “Well, there we go. At least I’ve got something to post on Xiaohongshu today.”
